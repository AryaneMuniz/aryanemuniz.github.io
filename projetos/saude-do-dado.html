The problemO problema

People asked every day to reprocess the branding data because "data was missing", even when the dashboard itself had run fine.Todo dia alguém pedia reprocessamento da base de branding porque "faltava dado", mesmo quando o dashboard tinha rodado certo.

What I builtO que eu fiz

Traced the gaps to their root cause at the source, by account and platform, and turned the diagnosis into a daily check. It's the foundation for an alerting agent that answers "is yesterday's data in?" before anyone asks for a rerun.Encontrei a causa das lacunas na origem, por conta e plataforma, e transformei o diagnóstico numa checagem diária. Ela é a base de um agente de alertas que responde "o dado de ontem entrou?" antes de alguém pedir reprocessamento.
